About us

Holliday's On-Demand Fuel Delivery

Holliday's On-Demand Fuel Delivery is a family-owned and operated service built from a legacy six generations strong. Rooted in the Tri-State and guided by a shared commitment to convenience and care, the company was created in response to one simple truth: life keeps getting busier, and time is one of our most valuable resources.

Born from old-school values, Holliday's proudly brings back the classic experience of having your fuel pumped for you — now with the ease and luxury of on-demand delivery. Whether at work or on the go, customers can count on dependable fueling without standing in the cold, making last-minute stops, or rushing through already hectic mornings.

Serving this community means serving neighbors, familiar faces, and the people who make the Tri-State the place we're proud to call home. As a family-owned company built by lifelong members of this region, Holliday's takes pride in fueling the community it knows best, guided by a deep respect for the value of your time. It's a principle at the center of every decision we make — and why we're committed to giving more of that time back to you.

FAQ

Questions about mobile fuel delivery

Is fuel delivery safe?

Yes. Our drivers are trained professionals who follow strict safety and regulatory standards on every delivery, using certified fueling equipment and vehicles.

Do I need to be present during delivery?

No. As long as we can safely access your vehicle and the fuel door is unlocked, we can complete your delivery during your selected time window.

What areas do you serve?

We currently serve drivers and fleets across the Ohio/Kentucky/West Virginia tri-state area.

How do I get pricing?

Real‑time per‑gallon prices and exact delivery fees are shown in the app before you confirm your order. Fleet customers can contact sales for custom plans.
